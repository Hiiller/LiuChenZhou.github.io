# LiuChen Zhou

**[Graduate] · [WuHan University]**

I work at the intersection of brain–computer interfaces and biomedical engineering. My research focuses on the quantitative assessment and modeling of hand motor function and Parkinson’s disease.

I am interested in combining neural signals, movement data, and machine learning to understand motor behavior and develop objective measures of neurological function.

## Research Interests

- **Brain–Computer Interfaces** — EEG analysis, neural signal processing, and brain–behavior relationships.
- **Hand Movement Quantification** — Fine-grained analysis of movement speed, amplitude, rhythm, hesitation, and temporal changes.
- **Parkinson’s Disease Assessment** — Quantitative modeling of motor symptoms using video and movement recordings.
- **Multimodal and Time-Series Learning** — Learning from neural signals and motion trajectories under limited supervision.

## Current Research

My current work explores how movement quality evolves over time and how models can learn meaningful motor representations from limited annotations. I also investigate connections between brain activity and observable movement in interdisciplinary engineering and clinical research.
